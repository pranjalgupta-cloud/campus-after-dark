About Campus After Dark

India's campus facility marketplace

Campus After Dark was built to solve a real problem: educational institutions across India have world-class infrastructure that sits idle in the evenings, weekends, and holidays — while organizations struggle to find affordable, professional venues.

We built a two-sided marketplace that connects verified educational institutions with verified external organizations. Our platform handles discovery, booking requests, availability management, transparent pricing with commission breakdown, demo payment flow, and analytics — all in one place.

Our Mission

To help Indian educational institutions maximize the social and economic value of their infrastructure while providing organizations access to premium, trusted venues.

Platform Model

We charge a configurable commission (currently 10%) on each confirmed booking. This is shown transparently to all parties. Institutions receive the remainder as payout.

This platform is currently in development/demo mode. All sample data is clearly labelled.
