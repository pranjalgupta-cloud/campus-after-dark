What is Campus After Dark?

Colleges and universities in India have world-class infrastructure — auditoriums, computer labs, sports fields — that sits idle after hours and on weekends. Campus After Dark unlocks that potential by connecting campuses (who earn rent) with businesses, NGOs, and event organisers (who need affordable venues).

🏫
For Colleges & Universities

List your unused spaces — auditoriums, labs, grounds — set your own pricing and availability, and start earning transparent revenue every time someone books them.

🏢
For Organisations & Renters

Search verified campus venues by city, type, and capacity. Submit a booking request, pay securely online, and use premium infrastructure at a fraction of hotel costs.
